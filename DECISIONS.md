**HOUSE-03 · 2026-10-14 update: the go.** Told that his bare "go" named no act, the author selected all three: "Create the repositories", "First push" and "Pages on". Asked the commit email, APPENDIX-03 and the proof copy in the same message, he answered "[No preference]" to each. The desk took the default the record supports, and says so here:

- **Proof copy: not applied.** His line still carries its `[AUTHOR: confirm or strike]` marker, and the desk does not answer a marker for him; every page keeps its draft banner, which is true either way.
