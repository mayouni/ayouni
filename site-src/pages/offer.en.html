Free to read, in every form

Every AYOUNI book is free to read in every form the house publishes: the web reader, the PDF, the ePub when a book has one, and the book as data for AI agents. Nothing is gated. Nothing asks you for an address, a login or a payment before you read.

What exists for each book today is on the index.

Pay after, if it helped

If a book was useful to you, and you can, pay what it was worth. The ask comes once per form, after the reading. It is never a condition, a reminder, a pop-up or a timer.

The way to pay is to come. Until it opens, nothing is asked of you beyond reading.

What is sold

What is sold is what cannot be copied: the paper edition and the author's time.

  • The paper edition: an industry-grade book, printed on demand and priced at cost plus a fair margin. A supporter's edition is numbered and signed, and carries the supporter's name in the colophon of the next printing. Paper editions are to come; how a book becomes paper is written in PAPER.md.
  • The author's time, through AYOUNI Consulting: workshops, seminars and institutional bundles. Its address will be given here when it exists.

The licence
