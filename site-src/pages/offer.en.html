Free to read, in every form

Every AYOUNI book is free to read in every form the house publishes: the web reader, the PDF, the ePub when a book has one, and the book as data for AI agents. Nothing is gated. Nothing asks you for an address, a login or a payment before you read.

What exists for each book today is on the index.

No payment

There is no payment in the books or on this site: no ask, no button, no bank details, no payment service. The open book is the mission, whole.

Working with the author

What is sold is the work around the books, to organisations: paper copies in quantity, workshops and training, talks and conference invitations, consulting and the building of solutions on the books' subjects. Amounts are discussed case by case with each customer, and payment follows ordinary banking.

The licence

The text is free to read and share, with credit to the author, with no commercial resale and no derivatives; translations and editions stay the author's, and so does the paper edition. That is Creative Commons BY-NC-ND 4.0. Code blocks follow the licence of the library they run against. The licence page says it for every book.
