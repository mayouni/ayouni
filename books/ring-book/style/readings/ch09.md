# Close reading — chapter 9, "A gamified foundation for mastering Ring" (99 pp., almost no code)

Read 2026-10-11 by a fresh reader (an agent given the published proof text, the Word text and `STYLE-BRIEF.md`, nothing else). Source note: the proof (published text) and the Word file differ; the copyeditor flattened several of the author's own moves. Where that matters both are quoted. Figures are images (garbled in the proof); nothing in them was recoverable except captions.

**0. The world, the cast, the rules (what this chapter is)**

*Mapping (§ Programming, Redefined!; § The Ringorialand Map).* "Programming is providing lights to someone in need." Water = data/names (Watermania's four isthmuses = Ring's four basic types); sand = behaviour (Bay of Sand: keywords, operators, standard functions, libraries); the Flower of Athingza = data modelling; the Ringuter millstone = the computer; recipes = programs; storz (stars) = the running output; lights = customer value (Health/Gold/Wisdom/Happiness, and "light of lights"); zex/spex/tex/yex = needs, requirements, technical design, UX; Royal Library = Ring libraries; Xtensia island = C/C++ extensions; Training Camp = nine steps, three levels = the book's nine chapters.

*Cast.* God Ringaros (four commandments: clean, strong, wise, beautiful); dead King Ringamesh (the 12-step Roadmap); Queen Ringafoye = CEO ("puts a ring on his finger"); Admiral Ringson = project manager ("captured by Ringson crusty eyes"); Princess Ringaliza = UX designer who sings; Ringayar the poet = business analyst, her forbidden lover; Ringshtein the Alchemist = architect; Ringapus the octopus = event monitor; Ringza the dragon = the language; Xos the Enemy = bad performance; miners = customer representatives; Ringorian/Ringuard/Ringler = iron/silver/gold rings.

*Rules (§ Rules of the Game; § Harvest the Stars).* Knowledge Points, Experience Points, Energy Points; stars buy rings; an "Advantage" for answers found in the Holy Book; "if it is terrible, it explodes!" and "the prison" for bad Big-O; "you won't be forgiven when you don't know one of them" (49 keywords, 208 functions). The actual scoring is deferred: "I have designed a complete scoring system that I will leave for the future" (fn 59).

*Does fiction stay in step with code?* Only one listing exists (§ Ringza Is a Visual Language, the 4-line factorial); it runs correctly but its comment says "multiply recursively" for a loop. Internally the fiction drifts: Ringza is "the Ring programming language" (§ Map) and later "Drakon is nothing but Ringza" (§ Visual Language) — he admits "a dose of constructive ambiguity"; Ringopus/Ringapus, storz/strorz, Titiey/Tititiey, Ringomeeter/Ringometer vary; § Cap of Lights numbers the naming skills 11–14 while Table 9-3 has them 21–24; the text promises "five groups" and the table has seven (two labelled VI). Teaching mapping is one-directional: each place gets a paragraph "In programming, ..." but no Ring construct is ever shown inside the fiction.

**1. Shape**

Opening: (1) a question answered in one word; (2–3) good vs master programmer, escalating to "a lifelong project you undertake"; then a barrage of nine "How can I…?" questions, a four-bullet agenda, a section-by-section roadmap, and "Nine Things You Will Learn". Each section pre-announces its own structure ("First, I discuss… Then, I analyze… Next, I propose… Finally"). Sub-sections of § Ringoria follow a fixed template: fantasy description → "See Figure" → "In programming, …" → a duty for the reader. Close: anecdote (Avionav), "I told you the truth", a command to repeat the ritual ("close your eyes" + fn "You must do it this time. Promise?"), then a detached Tuareg parable the copyeditor asked to delete (AU44). Prose:code ≈ 99:1; 56 figures, 3 tables, 67 footnotes.

**2. Teaching moves**

Definition hunt before his own definition: § Defining the Word Metaphor stacks Iraq greeting, Aristotle, Cicero, Merriam-Webster, Elvis, Arabic "Majaaz Mursal", cognitive linguistics, then "we define metaphor as…". Problem-tree "Why?" chain "adopting the behavior of a five-year-old child". Names things before explaining them and says so: "There will be new words… you have never heard before. Read ahead" (fn 26). Confusion is pre-empted by a footnote, not by rewriting. Limits of the method are stated: "These steps form a happy path you will probably never meet in real life."

**3. Voice**

"I" dominates (essay, "my essay", "In my experience" ×4, "I swear"); "we" for shared deductions; "you" is the apprentice ("dear friend"). Grave register on people: "slightly incompetent project managers… slightly incompetent business analysts" (§ Iron Compass). Playful footnotes undercut commands: "I know you didn't obey, but that's OK" (fn 27); "I agree this is an exaggeration. I'm sorry" (fn 37); "Programmers are notoriously lazy, aren't they?" (fn 62). Self-interrupting questions: "You know what?", "Simple, right?", "Did I say teamcards?", "What about gaming?". Exclamations closing paragraphs: "complexity!", "the Ringler!", "the prison." The Word opening had "You are right: technical expertise. But!" — the proof removed it.

**4. Images**

Load-bearing: water/sand/millstone/lights (the whole Ring mapping), the problem tree, the programming cube, sailor/ship ("a sailor on a program ship in a hectic programming sea"), Heartland/Mackinder. Decorative: "fruit cocktail of code, culture, ethics", "ifs and thens flourishing on the wall like climbing herbs", "social sauce", "mastering the bull by simply maintaining its horns". Personal/cultural: Tunisian football café, Gafsa millstone "still in use in my rural village", Tunisian insults Bâlbâz/Fêgfêg/Mîîda ("Excuse me for this vulgarity"), Quran 25:53 for Watermania, Coelho, Lao Tzu, Dalai Lama, Tuareg veil, Libyan "zenga zenga", daughters and wife as co-authors (fn 1), "From Niamey to Miami". Metaphors are introduced by glossary, sustained by template, dropped at section ends ("That's the end of the trip").

**5. Sentence craft**

Long cumulative sentences with triads: "clean, strong, wise, and beautiful"; "sunny days but rainy nights, flying birds but hungry sharks". Repetition as emphasis: "negotiate, and negotiate, and negotiate!", "changing changes", "Repeat it again, and again". Openers: "In programming," (≥12), "In Ringoria," (≥10), "In my experience," "Still,", "Thus,", "That said,", "Finally,", "Now,". Colon definitions; brackets for asides; quotation marks on invented terms. Characteristic words: metaphor (~90), Ringler (~60), mastery/master (~50), complexity, performance, change, energy, "real-world" (~20), "serious game", "root cause". Tics: "simply" / "just" ("just wasting your time, just wasting your energy"), unsourced absolutes ("the root cause of all problems in software" — flagged AU3), self-advertising ("maybe for the first time" ×3), hedged apologies after strong claims.

**6. Structure of explanation**

Principle first, then story, then rule. Tables used as instruments (Table Complete with ••• self-rating), figures as the argument (problem tree in five stages). Facts half-sourced: tinyurl footnotes, Wikipedia, Dan Roam quoted, but "I don't have the title of the book yet. I've contacted the author on LinkedIn" (fn 57). Honesty markers: "That's my point of view, not a scientific truth" (fn 25), "To the best of my knowledge", "Some propositions are strong enough, but some aren't yet."

**7. Exemplary passages**

- § Programming, Redefined!: "Close your eyes. You are teleported to a fantasy world called Ringorialand. Now, open your eyes. Programming is providing lights to someone in need."
- § An Iron Compass: "Raise your head from the dunes of code, look high to the stars, take a deep breath, and find where you are on the globe!"
- § Ringza by Heart, Ringza by Hand!: "Ringza is a dragon. Ringza is the voice of wisdom. Ringza is the language. Three in one, and for a reason."

Weakest, § A Critic: "its main drawback is that it enforces the illusion of mastering the bull by simply maintaining its horns, osculating all the differences" — mixed idiom plus wrong word (occulting), the claim rests on nothing shown.

**8. What an AI would fake badly**

Easy to fake: Ring-prefixed names, "In programming,…" templates, exclamation-closed paragraphs. Easy to overdo: footnote jokes and apologies. Hard-to-fake core: the apology-after-overclaim rhythm born of real conviction; a lived North-African/Sahel reference stock (Gafsa, Niamey, Tuareg) used without explanation; and family as co-authors of the fiction. Also note the copyedit removed his best flourishes ("furious relationship with programming", "inviting fantasy to the programming desert, many palms arose") — the Word file, not the proof, is the truer sample of voice.
