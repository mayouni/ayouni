This book is about one discipline: governing the change to software, now that writing the change is no longer the slow part of the work. I call it refinement-oriented programming, and I tell it through a bank that receives a letter.

**The bank is invented.** Softibank, its people and its letters are fiction. The central bank, the monetary union, the city and the currency are real. Chapter 1 says so once, and the glossary says it again.

**The code is not.** Every block in this book runs against the open Softanza library, at a commit named in full, `93d7a39bd`, the same commit my Softanza book runs at. Each block is followed by the exact answer it printed, and the book's build runs every block again and refuses a page whose answer changed. A block marked as an exercise is yours to run; I do not print its answer.

**The language is Haro.** The blocks are written in it, in three forms: the classic form, which names the object on every line; the chain, for one sentence; and the scene, `obj { ... }`, where three or more things are said to one object.

**Every chapter says where it stops.** Its first lines name what is **built** (the code exists and a block ran), what is **designed** (a document says so, and nothing runs), and what is **absent**. Each chapter ends with a limit and with something left in your hands: a defect, an open question, a rule to write.

**The book stands on another.** *Softanza — The Governed Software Platform of the AI Age* is the foundation, and this book cites its chapters rather than repeat them. Refine, the product built on that foundation, has a part of its own at the end, as stage and direction.

**The parts.** Part I, the discipline: the letter, what broke, the unit of change. Part II, the gate, run: the point, the gate, the cascade, the timeline, trust and governance. Part III, the AI agent at the point. Part IV, Softibank's eighteen months, to the auditor's visit. Part V, Refine. A coda, and a glossary.

Read it in order, with the library open beside you. Run each block before you read what I say about it.
