# Voice reader: 02-what-broke.md

Reader: a fresh voice reader (brief `writing-framework/briefs/voice-reader.md`), 2026-10-15. Nothing changed in the page.

## 1. Shape (C9)

Opening: first person, three sentences, a roadmap. One verb jars: "Then I run the list of what a change must carry" (a list is not run; the block is).

Section closes: **The afternoon** closes on a question beat ("who makes sure it is right?"), a hand-over. **The field** closes on "both sides of the desk held people", a verdict that hands over. **The surface, declared** closes on "The program says what it was built to have changed." **Where the field leads** closes on "the next paragraph says so": it is the next section, a mechanical hand-over.

Chapter close: a bridge ("The next chapter says what replaced it, and why the replacement had to be typed."). Not a summary.

## 2. Run, then read (C1)

The first block arrives at about forty per cent of the page. Before it: **The afternoon**, **The field** and **The principle**, three sections of argument and a five-item list, with no run. The list of five is explained before the run that shows it; the run then reads the list back. The worst abstract paragraph (**The principle**): "When writing speeds up and reading does not, review does not stop. It goes on in form" — no figure within reach. The scene (forty changes, twenty minutes) rescues it in part.

## 3. The defect (C2)

The exercise is partly a demonstration (propose to each point, predict which properties show), with a real gap named at its end: "`Why()` answers only for the last proposal" and "that gap is the one chapter 12's auditor would ask about." The defect is real and named as unfinished. No wrong-then-right on this page. No correct code rejected for taste.

## 4. Where it stops (C3)

No "weak" or "falls short". One sentence leans toward underselling: "I leave it open: it is the place where this discipline has the most work still to do." (**In your hands**). Direction would serve better than a ranking ("it is the next step of the timeline").

The limit is present, staged, and prints the loss beside the win (**Where the field leads**: reach and memory). Good.

Reach:
- "In a few years, writing code went from the most expensive step in making software to one of the cheapest." Marked as view two sentences later; acceptable, and it is a superlative pair.
- "Every earlier era of programming had the same slow step: writing." (**The afternoon**): unbacked absolute.
- "a question arrived in every serious software team at once" (**The afternoon**): unbacked absolute.
- "That sentence is the hinge of this book." (**The afternoon**): self-advertising.

## 5. The particulars (C4)

Softibank, Amina, the auditor at month eighteen, fourteen products, "an afternoon": all in `manuscript/rop-book.md`. "forty changes", "the twenty minutes the old symmetry assumed" (**The principle**): composed figures, said as illustration; they read as numbers. Mark them as an example or drop the precision. "in the teams I see" and "the work I see": the author's witness, put in his mouth; check that he owns it (it is a claim of his experience). No `[AUTHOR` marker.

## 6. Metaphors (C5)

- "the afternoon" (heading and refrain: "the speed of the afternoon", "match an afternoon with attention"): load-bearing, the book's figure for the new speed; stable. Good.
- "both sides of the desk held people": carries the symmetry; keep.
- "the hinge of this book": decorative.
- "The name is still under the change. It certifies less.": a figure that works.

## 7. Address (C6)

I: about 9 ("In my view" twice, "I see" twice, "I refused" twice, "I leave it open"). You: about 6. We: 0. Questions: 2, answered on the page. Exclamations: 0 (none earned; a run here could carry one). The tool speaks: "The change and its point answered for them".

## 8. Rhythm and noise (C7, §4)

Long-then-short at its best: "Judging authors does not keep pace with an afternoon. Judging changes can." Colons: about 15; em dashes: 0. (**The field**), third time with chapters 1 and 3. Absolutes listed in item 4. No forbidden word; "AI agent", "AI models" exact.

## 9. Counted sets (C8)

"four instruments": diff, pull request, sign-off, version control: 4. "five things": validated, scoped, attributed, reversible, explainable: 5. "none of the three": who, what checked, what reached: 3. Order drift: **The five, carried** reads them as Validated, Explainable, Scoped, Attributed, Reversible, not the order the list just set. Reading in the run's order is defensible; say so, or keep the list's order.

## 10. The stake (C10)

"a discipline built on judging them would have to be rebuilt each time. A discipline built on judging changes holds." (**What I refused**): conviction, unmarked, backed by the run.

## 11. Most him, least him

Least him: "That sentence is the hinge of this book. The draft would not be careless work." (**The afternoon**). An agent's signpost: it tells the reader the sentence matters instead of letting the next paragraph show it.
