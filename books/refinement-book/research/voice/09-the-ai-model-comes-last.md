# Voice reading: 09 The AI model comes last

Fresh reader, brief `writing-framework/briefs/voice-reader.md`, 2026-10-15. Nothing changed in the chapter.

## 1. Shape (C9)

Opening: first person, four sentences, a roadmap in three steps (rules first, AI model last, its words a proposal). Kept. "the question everyone asks first" is reach (see 4); chapter 8's close used the same "everyone is asking about".

Section closes: "The rules first" closes on a verdict ("exactly right is what rules do."); "The AI model, last" closes on the division of labour ("the person did what the gate cannot"). Both hand over.

Chapter close: "The AI model comes last, and its words are proposals." restates the title and principle, then bridges to chapter 10. One sentence of summary before the bridge; the bridge alone would carry it.

## 2. Run, then read (C1)

The run arrives late: two provenance paragraphs stand between the principle and the first block. Worst: "The Teaching Edition of this book took that loop apart on a ladder: a pattern, a reflex, a thinking machine, an agent." (The field), then "The sentence is adapted from the Softanza book's chapter 17, which says 'a model' and 'the court'..." (The principle). Both are apparatus about other books, with no run within reach; the second reads as an agent's citation note, not the author.

## 3. The defect (C2)

Real: the third draft, "A draft the program cannot read never reaches the gate at all", with a rule to write and its place named ("before the gate, in the program that receives the draft"). Wrong before right: the slip `0.61` is shown and refused before the corrected draft. Good. No correct code rejected for taste.

## 4. Where it stops (C3)

Undersell: none. "which a real draft would break in many ways" is a limit told as direction ("holding a draft to it here, before the gate, is the direction"). The absence of a called AI model is said twice, in the run section and the limit: honest and placed.

Reach: "it reaches the same conclusion every time it runs, at no cost, offline" (The rules first, "at no cost" is larger than the page shows); "That is language work, and it is what AI models are for." (The AI model, last, an absolute); "the question everyone asks first" (opening).

## 5. The particulars (C4)

Softibank's three products `auto_loan`, `school_fees`, `harvest` ("three of Softibank's products"): trace to `docs/characters-and-grounding.md`; if not there, they are composed. "A new circular arrives in French prose": a story particular; trace. "sixty-one per cent": declared as written by hand for the run, fine. Softanza book chapters 9, 17, 19, 20: cross-references for the verifier. No `[AUTHOR` markers; no composed memory.

## 6. Metaphors (C5)

"ladder" (The field): the Teaching Edition's, named in passing and never mapped here; decorative on this page. "mind" and "faculty" (Principle): carried from the Softanza book, stable. "door" (The limit, "the platform's door for AI agents"): consistent with chapter 10. No sustained new metaphor.

## 7. Address (C6)

I: about 9 (opening 2, "My Softanza book", "I do not repeat", "I keep", two "I refused", "the defect I leave you"). We: 0. You: 3 ("you may choose it per task", exercise imperatives). Questions: "Where does an AI model belong, then? Where rules cannot go." (The AI model, last), a beat answered at once; the exercise's question is left to the reader as the defect, rightly. Exclamations: 0 in about 1,600 words; the refusal of `0.61` is a reveal that could carry one.

## 8. Rhythm and noise (C7, §4)

Long-then-short present: "Fluency is not a check.", "That is admission over generation." Colons, no dash. Intensifiers: 0. Formula: "This is general knowledge" twice (The field; Where the field leads), a recurring agent disclaimer across chapters 8 to 11. Absolutes: "Nothing in it needs an AI model, and nothing in it should have one." (strong, his kind); "it is what AI models are for". A technical caveat sits inside a refusal: "Governance passes here because none is declared, as chapter 2 said." (What I refused), out of place in a bold "I refused" paragraph.

## 9. Counted sets (C8)

"Read the three answers in order": three. "Three proposals, three admissions, three steps": matches. "the same five checks": five. The ladder: four rungs listed, no count stated. Names exact.

## 10. The stake (C10)

"A bank's penalty cap is work that must be exactly right, and exactly right is what rules do." (The rules first). Backed by the loop just run; the general clause is a conviction, unmarked. "the person did what the gate cannot, which is to read the circular and decide" is backed by the run.

## 11. Most him, least him

Most him:
- "Where does an AI model belong, then? Where rules cannot go. A new circular arrives in French prose, and someone must read what it implies for the bank's products." (The AI model, last)
- "Read the three answers in order. The slip is refused by the point's own bounds, before anyone asks where it came from." (The AI model, last)
- "That is admission over generation. The AI model generated; the gate admitted or refused; and the person did what the gate cannot..." (The AI model, last)

Least him: "The sentence is adapted from the Softanza book's chapter 17, which says 'a model' and 'the court' where this book says an AI model and the gate; this chapter runs it at a bank." (The principle). A provenance note about terminology, addressed to an editor, not to the reader at the keyboard.
