## 4. Where it stops (C3)

Stage words named and defined: built, designed, absent. One definition reads as underselling (AWF-08): "what is **designed** (a document says so, and nothing runs)" (**Every chapter says where it stops.**). True only if the reader build prints the `stage` block at the head of each page. "Each chapter ends with a limit and with something left in your hands": each chapter ends on a bridge, after those two. Small, but a promise the page should keep exactly.
