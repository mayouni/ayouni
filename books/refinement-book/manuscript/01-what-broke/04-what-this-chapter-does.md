# §1.4 What this chapter does

The remainder of this chapter establishes the present situation. What happened to software development between 2024 and 2026 to make the existing review architecture untenable. What the published evidence from the major AI labs now confirms about the nature of the bottleneck. What the difference is between writing-acceleration tools and review-and-governance tools, and why the latter category turns out to be the more durable one.

Chapter Two will then take up the question of the unit of change — what is wrong with the text diff as the atom of software work, and what replaces it. The remainder of Part One develops the diagnosis. Parts Two and Three develop the discipline. Part Four shows the discipline at work in the production environment that grounded its design. Part Five examines what the discipline implies for the broader practice of software, and where its limits begin.

> **How to read.** If you are a programmer, the book is structured so that any chapter can be read independently. If you are a compliance officer, a regulator, or a banking analyst with no programming background, you may skip the code listings in Part Two with no loss of comprehension. The narrative thread — Amina's refinement, from Tuesday in September to the auditor's visit eighteen months later — runs through every chapter and stands on its own.
